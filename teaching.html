Thesis Supervision

I supervise Bachelor’s and Master’s theses in applied microeconomics, particularly in public economics and gender economics. The number of theses I can supervise is limited, and I may therefore not be able to accept every request. Students should approach me with an initial topic or research idea that falls within my research areas. We can then refine the research question and develop the project together. Please contact me via email and attach your current transcript and, if possible, a short exposé outlining the proposed topic.
